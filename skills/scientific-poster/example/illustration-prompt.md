# Nano Banana prompt for the panel 1 graphic

Result saved as `poster/assets/problem.jpeg` (3584 x 1184); `problem.png` is the same image with the white background made transparent. The build uses the PNG when present, else the JPEG.

## Prompt

Flat vector-style scientific illustration, wide 3:1 banner, pure white background, no text, no labels, no watermark.

Three stages from left to right, connected by two thin arrows in muted blue (#5B7FB5).

Left: a clean 2D chemical structure drawing of a small bicyclic molecule in the style of ChemDraw, crisp black bond lines, red oxygen atoms, bold wedge and hashed bonds for stereochemistry, drawn on a simple document icon with a folded corner, the document outlined in teal (#096779).

Middle: the same molecule printed inside an open journal page, but heavily pixelated and slightly blurred, as a low-resolution raster image, the page outlined in pale blue (#B9C7E0).

Right: a magnifying glass in dark navy (#072563) hovering over an empty database cylinder, with a large magenta (#C61354) question mark inside the lens.

Minimal, geometric, generous white space, consistent 4 px line weight, flat colours only, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no people, no hands. Colours limited to navy #072563, magenta #C61354, teal #096779, pale blue #B9C7E0, black and white.

## Variant (if the first is too busy)

Flat vector icon trio on white, 3:1, no text: a ChemDraw-style molecule drawing with wedge bonds inside a teal document icon; an arrow; the same molecule as a coarse pixel mosaic inside a pale blue picture frame; an arrow; a dark navy magnifying glass with a magenta question mark. Thin uniform lines, flat colours (#072563, #C61354, #096779, #B9C7E0), lots of white space.
